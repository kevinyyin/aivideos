# Caption

The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower 🗼💸

Why would a careful businessman hand a stranger a fortune for a tower nobody can own? In 1925, con man Victor Lustig posed as an official from France's Ministry of Posts and Telegraphs and told Paris's top scrap dealers that the rusting Eiffel Tower was being sold for scrap, in secret. André Poisson paid, Lustig fled to Austria with about 70,000 francs, and the trick that sealed it was a bribe: only a real official would dare to ask for one.
