# Caption

The Engineers Who Saved Pisa by Digging Under It 🏛️⛏️

How do you stop a 14,500-tonne tower from falling over? Cement pumped into its foundation in 1934 made the Leaning Tower of Pisa lurch south, and freezing the ground in 1995 made it lurch again, overnight. So between 1999 and 2001, engineers pulled 38 cubic metres of soil out from under the side that wasn't sinking, and the top swung back 45 cm.

Would you have let them dig under it? 👇
