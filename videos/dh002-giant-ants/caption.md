# Caption

What if every ant on Earth woke up the size of a Labrador? 🐜🐕

There are about 20 quadrillion ants, roughly 2.4 million for every person, and they can lift 10 to 50 times their own weight. So the fridge goes first. Then the car, the wedding cake, and the picnic… until we figure out they make pretty great neighbors.

Would you keep one as a pet? 👇
