# Caption

What if every "6-7" got twice as loud? 🔊🤚🤚

Leo whispers it once in class. Every "6-7" after that is +10 dB, about twice as loud to your ears, and nobody can stop saying it back: the class, the whole school, a stadium, a parrot on a power line. By #18 it's past 194 dB, the loudest sound air can carry. Then it blows up the Moon. Then the Sun.

Could you go one day without saying it? 👇
