# Caption

The Bird That Beat an Army 🪖🐦

How did Australia lose a war to emus? In 1932, Major Meredith took two machine guns and 10,000 rounds into Western Australia's wheat fields to stop 20,000 hungry emus. The flock split into tiny groups, a gun jammed, the truck couldn't keep up, and after six days the army pulled out while Parliament joked about medals. Machine guns need an enemy standing in a line, and emus don't do lines.

Should the emus get the medals? 👇
