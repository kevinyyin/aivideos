# Caption

Isaac Newton Went Undercover to Save England's Money 🪙🔍

Isaac Newton spent his nights in disguise in London's roughest taverns, and the reason is still on the edge of your quarter. In the 1690s English coins were real silver, so people shaved slivers off the edges until a bag of money weighed barely half what it should. Newton's Mint re-struck the money with ridged edges that show any shave, and he chased the forger William Chaloner, who had mocked him in Parliament, all the way to a treason verdict. Today's quarters have no silver at all, but they still carry 119 ridges.
