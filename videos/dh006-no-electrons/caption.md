# Caption

What if every electron on Earth went on a 5-second break? ⚡🌭

You're on a busy street at noon when it happens: the sky goes black, nothing reflects light (not even your hands), and within a femtosecond every molecule falls apart. The bare nuclei fly off at nearly light speed with roughly 300 billion trillion trillion times the energy it takes to blow up Earth (napkin math). Somehow, one hot dog makes it.

Which particle should vanish next? 👇
Send this to the friend who'd survive as the hot dog.
